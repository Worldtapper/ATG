STRUCTURED DELIVERY

OUR FABRICATION PROCESS.

A disciplined, transparent engineering methodology ensuring flawless translation from concept to finished installation.

01

Discovery & Site Survey

In-depth architectural review, 3D laser site measurement, and structural load feasibility assessment by our senior engineering team.

02

CAD Modelling & Engineering

Production of comprehensive technical shop drawings, finite element analysis (FEA), and material swatch sign-off before manufacturing.

03

Workshop Fabrication

Precision laser cutting, certified welding, and specialized surface treatment under stringent multi-point quality control protocols.

04

Site Rigging & Commissioning

White-glove site logistics, precision crane rigging, alignment calibration, and final client sign-off with operational training.
