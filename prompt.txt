Customers should be able to browse ATG’s metalwork, furniture, fabrication and interior product designs through clear categories.

The experience should work like a premium online furniture store:

1. The customer enters the Services page.
2. They browse the category navigation.
3. They select the category they are interested in.

5. Each design card displays its main image, name and price or quotation status.

7. They can save designs to the existing Favourites system.
8. They can add selected designs to the existing Start Your Project enquiry.

**This is an expansion of my existing Services page—not a replacement website.**

# 9. PRICING AND MATERIALS ACCURACY

Do not invent exact prices or represent an estimate as an official ATG quotation.

For every design, use one of these pricing formats:

- Verified fixed price.
- Defensible starting price.
- Defensible price range.
- Request a Quote.

### Estimation requirements

If an estimated price is included:

- Research relevant materials.
- Consider dimensions and complexity.
- Consider fabrication and finishing.
- Consider installation or delivery if relevant.
- Use a reasonable, defensible estimate.
- Store the assumptions in the catalogue data.
- Clearly label it as an estimate.

If the price cannot be estimated reliably, use:

**Request a Quote**

Do not block a design from being displayed simply because its final price is unknown.

### Materials requirements

Provide realistic materials where sufficient information exists.

Possible materials include:

- Mild steel.
- Stainless steel.
- Aluminium.
- Tempered glass.
- Hardwood.
- MDF or suitable engineered wood.
- Upholstery.
- Powder-coated metal finish.
- Stainless steel finish.
- Wood veneer.

For concept designs, use **Suggested Materials** rather than claiming the materials are confirmed for an actual ATG product.

If the exact materials cannot be determined confidently, state that final material selection is subject to confirmation.
