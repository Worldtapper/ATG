Featured Case Study

The Obsidian Residence

Client Challenge

To construct a structurally sound but visually weightless glass pavilion suspended over a steep cliff face, requiring absolute precision in structural steel fabrication.

Engineering Solution

We utilized aerospace-grade aluminium bracing and a custom-fabricated steel cantilever system that was assembled on-site with zero tolerance for error.

Final Outcome

An award-winning architectural marvel that seamlessly blends industrial strength with minimalist luxury, featuring panoramic views unobstructed by conventional supports.
